## Good to know

- Whatever you enter once is suggested again next time – including your own mixes such as nettle tea.
- The log list shows every feeding with all products at a glance.
- NPK basics, dosing and feeding plans are covered in our [fertilizing guide](/en/blog/how-to-fertilize-plants/).
