steps:
  - name: "Create a task"
    text: "Tap <strong>+</strong> at the bottom right and then <strong>Add Task</strong>. Pick the plant and enter a title, e.g. \u201cWater\u201d."
  - name: "Choose a category"
    text: "Pick water, feed, check, training or note. With <strong>Feed</strong> you can enter the planned fertilizer with brand and dose right away."
  - name: "Set the repetition"
    text: "Under <strong>Repeat (hours)</strong> enter how often the task comes back \u2013 48 means every 2 days, 168 once a week. <strong>Repeat until</strong> ends the series on a date."
  - name: "Turn on push reminders"
    text: "Open <strong>Account</strong> and enable <strong>Push reminders</strong>. Allow notifications when the browser asks. \u201cSend test\u201d checks it immediately."
  - name: "Complete a task"
    text: "Tick off due tasks in the calendar or the task list. Watering tasks create a watering entry automatically, feeding tasks open the pre-filled fertilizer log."
faq:
  - q: "Why am I not getting reminders?"
    a: "Check that push reminders are on under Account and that the browser allows notifications for app.sowling.com. On iPhone, push only works once Sowling has been added to the home screen."
  - q: "Can I end a series early?"
    a: "Yes. Edit the task and set an earlier \u201cRepeat until\u201d date, or delete it."

## Good to know

- Reminders also work with the app closed when it is installed. See [install the app](/en/guides/install-the-app/).
- On holiday, vacation mode extends watering intervals automatically.
- How often each plant needs water is explained in our [watering schedule guide](/en/blog/watering-schedule/).
