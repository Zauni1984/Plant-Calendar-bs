## Signing in

After e-mail and password, Sowling asks for the current code from the app. A recovery code works instead of the code. After ten wrong codes, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.

## Turning it off

Under **Account → Two-factor sign-in → Turn off**, with your password and a current code.
