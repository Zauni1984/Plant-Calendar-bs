# Right of withdrawal

**Last updated: 23 November 2026**

Consumers have a statutory right of withdrawal for contracts concluded with us
via the Sowling website, web app or desktop app (the Basic, Pro and Nursery
subscriptions and the one-time purchase of the desktop app). A consumer is any
natural person who enters into a transaction for purposes that are
predominantly outside their trade, business or profession (§ 13 German Civil
Code, BGB).

## Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without
giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day the contract is concluded.

## Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments
received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen
days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from
this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of
payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly
agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such
reimbursement.

If you requested the service (subscription) to begin during the withdrawal
period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been
provided until you have communicated to us your withdrawal from this contract,
in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

## Early expiry of the right of withdrawal

**Desktop app (one-time purchase):** For a contract for the supply of digital
content not on a tangible medium (licence key and activation of the desktop
app), the right of withdrawal expires once we have begun performance after you

1. expressly consented to performance starting before the end of the
   withdrawal period,
2. acknowledged that you lose your right of withdrawal once performance has
   begun, and
3. received a confirmation of the contract from us (e.g. by e-mail)

(§ 356(5) BGB).

**Subscriptions:** For a service contract, the right of withdrawal expires
once we have fully performed the service, if performance only began after you
gave your express consent and at the same time acknowledged that you lose your
right of withdrawal once the contract has been fully performed (§ 356(4) BGB).

## Cancelling subscriptions

Independently of the right of withdrawal, you can cancel subscriptions at any
time for the end of the current billing period – in the app under
**Subscription** via the Stripe customer portal.
